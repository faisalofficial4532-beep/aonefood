## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end, cinematic dark fast-casual dining experience. It transforms quick culinary ordering into a multi-sensory, premium ritual—bridging the immediacy of modern fast-casual logistics with the mood, warmth, and atmospheric reverence of flagship nighttime hospitality.

### Brand Personality & Mood
- **Cinematic & Seductive:** Charcoal depths, warm ember tones, and focused illumination that spotlight culinary craft like stage lighting.
- **Opulent yet Accessible:** High-contrast editorial elegance balanced by intuitive, zero-friction ordering interactions.
- **Warm & Appetizing:** Rich roasted spice undertones, avoiding cold digital blacks in favor of charred espresso and warm ember pigments.

### Design Aesthetic
The aesthetic draws on tactile nocturnal minimalism infused with modern luxury glassmorphism. Deep, roasted charcoal canvas surfaces are layered with softly blurred overlays, translucent navigation ribbons, subtle saffron rim highlights, and atmospheric radial backlights that cradle featured dishes.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around roasted heat, deep smoke, and glowing hearthside warmth, maintaining strict WCAG AA contrast against rich dark backdrops.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#F26A0F`):** Flame Orange. Used for focal call-to-actions, cart badges, active indicators, and high-impact micro-interactions.
  - Interactive Hover: `#FF8A1F`
  - Interactive Active: `#D45500`
- **Secondary (`#E9B44C`):** Saffron Gold. Used for membership tiers, culinary highlights, chef recommendations, and delicate rim lighting accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D62828`):** Crimson Ember. Reserved strictly for limited-time promotions, fiery spice badges, urgency counters, and stock warnings.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#14100D`):** Deep Char. A warm espresso-tinted black that anchors the viewport, preventing the clinical sterility of pure pitch black.
- **Surface Elevation Cards (`#1F1814`):** Charcoal Roast. Applied to food card surfaces, dialogue sheets, and item cards.
- **Structural Borders (`#2D241E`):** Toasted Umber. Subtle dividers and outlines that provide crisp edge definition under low light.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - High Emphasis: Cream White (`#FFF6EC`) for headings and core text.
  - Medium Emphasis: Muted Cream (`#C8BEB5`) for supporting descriptions, metadata, and inactive icons.
  - Low Emphasis: Ash Cocoa (`#736357`) for placeholders and disabled states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an intentional tension between editorial prestige and frictionless transactional clarity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Handled exclusively by `Playfair Display`. It brings a bespoke, restaurant-critic editorial weight to dish names, culinary narratives, and hero section banners. It elevates menu titles into signature plates.
- **User Interface & Body Text:** Governed strictly by `Inter`. With optimized optical clarity and vertical metrics, Inter ensures speed, readability, and immediate comprehension for prices, ingredient tags, modifiers, nutrition facts, and checkouts.
- **Accents:** Secondary script flourishes (such as seasonal callouts or the brand's signature catchphrases) may be deployed for non-functional decorative motifs, always paired directly alongside legible Inter labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs a fluid responsive grid with intentional vertical staging to present culinary offerings like curated showcase pieces.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of 1360px. Outer margins sit at `2rem` (`margin`), with column gaps at `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with dynamic margins of `1.5rem` and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with fixed outer padding of `1rem` (`margin-mobile`) and gutters of `1rem` (`gutter-mobile`).

### Spacing Principles
- Density increases around transactional components (item selectors, order summary sidebars, and modifier pills) using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Food photography and signature banners are afforded breath with `space-lg` and `space-xl` to reinforce luxury framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold artificial drop shadows, relying instead on ambient warm light scattering, roasted tonal layers, and glass-blurred surface materials.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Floor Base:** Ground canvas starts at `#14100D`.
- **Card Tier 1:** Raised content cards and interactive tiles sit at `#1F1814` with a crisp outer border of `#2D241E`.
- **Card Tier 2 (Floating):** Popovers, dropdown menus, and cart summaries sit at `#261E19`.

### Lighting & Ambient Glows
- **Radial Plate Glows:** High-priority culinary dishes are placed in front of an ambient pseudo-element radial gradient: `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(242, 106, 15, 0.16) 0%, rgba(20, 16, 13, 0) 70%)`.
- **Saffron Rim Accents:** Elevated selection states feature an inset golden rim highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(233, 180, 76, 0.25)`.
- **Backdrop Frosting:** Sticky navigation ribbons and drawer handles utilize a frosted glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(20, 16, 13, 0.82)`) bordered along the baseline by a hairline `#2D241E` divider.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`), ensuring interactive components feel welcoming without sacrificing architectural structure.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to input fields, notification badges, modifier chips, and smaller utility buttons.
- **Large Panels & Food Cards (1rem / 16px):** Standard radius for culinary item cards, promo sheets, modal frames, and meal builder wrappers.
- **Pill Geometry:** Used exclusively for category filters, counter tags, and status ribbons (e.g., "Flame Grilled", "Chef's Special") to provide clean ergonomic affordances.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#F26A0F`, text `#FFF6EC`, font `Inter` 600. Features subtle edge rounding (`0.5rem`), zero border, and hover state shifting to `#FF8A1F` with a soft orange ember drop shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(242, 106, 15, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #2D241E`, text `#FFF6EC`. On hover, the border brightens to `#E9B44C` with text subtly illuminated.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#C8BEB5`. Shifter on hover to `#FFF6EC` over `rgba(255, 246, 236, 0.04)`.

### Food & Product Cards
- **Structure:** Charcoal surface `#1F1814`, rounded corners (`1rem`), encapsulated by a subtle `1px solid #2D241E` border.
- **Visual Presentation:** Dish imagery bleeds to top edges or rests over centered soft flame-orange radial backlights. Price tags use `Inter` 600 in saffron gold (`#E9B44C`), while titles are set in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`).
- **Interactive States:** On hover, cards subtly translateY by -4px with border highlight transitioning from `#2D241E` to `rgba(242, 106, 15, 0.4)`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Default State:** Charcoal base `#1F1814`, outline `#2D241E`, text `#C8BEB5`, pill shape.
- **Selected State:** Solid flame orange `#F26A0F`, text `#FFF6EC`, accompanied by a warm diffused halo.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Dark roasted fill `#1A1410`, border `1px solid #2D241E`, text `#FFF6EC`, placeholder `#736357`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#F26A0F` accompanied by an ambient glow ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(242, 106, 15, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `20px` square container with `0.25rem` radius, bordered in `#2D241E`. Checked state transitions to `#F26A0F` background with an off-white `#FFF6EC` checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circular ring with saffron/orange filled center pip on active selection.

### Badges & Ribbons
- **Promo & Spice Tags:** Crimson red `#D62828` badge with bold uppercase text (`label-sm`) in `#FFF6EC`.
- **Prestige / Signature Indicator:** Saffron gold `#E9B44C` subtle border, dark background, gold text, conveying culinary prestige.
